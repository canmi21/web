## Question

The system had an accent, `oklch(0.623 0.214 259.8)` in both themes, and the focus ring drew in it;
information had a blue of its own a few degrees off. Which blue does the system keep, and how is a
ring told apart from a blue mark it is drawn on, without a gap around it?

## Options

In the order tried. The gap between ring and information is APCA's Lc counted before its clip at
10, the ring's lightness moved in its own hue at OKHSL saturation 100.

1. **Lc 25 apart**, then **15**: a second blue, standing too far off.
2. **Lc 5 apart, each toward its theme's ground**: the dark's ring under information's dark, the
   light's ring over information's light, `oklch(0.674 0.173 258.6)`. The pair's two values stood
   15.1 OKHSL steps apart where every other hue's stand 9.
3. **Lc 5 apart, darker in both themes**: the light's ring `oklch(0.626 0.203 258.6)`, the pair 9
   apart again, and white on it, a selection, reads better than on the lighter one.

## Decision

Option 3.

- **The ring is information's hue a shade darker in both themes, Lc 5**: `oklch(0.626 0.203 258.6)`
  in the light, `oklch(0.543 0.207 258.6)` in the dark. Told apart by lightness, never by a gap.
- **There is no accent.** What was the system's own and live is information's blue, held by
  `--status-info` as every other status holds its hue; `--interaction-focus` keeps its name and
  draws a shade off it.
- **A selection is drawn as focus is**: `--interaction-focus` solid with white on it, since a
  selection is the reader's own choice as focus is. The palette's selection had been its ramp's far
  end under the text, which went gray in both themes.
