## Question

Every status hue needs a value in each theme. The palette's own pairs differed by hand: the dark a
little lighter, by amounts that varied, warning the same in both. What rule derives a hue's pair, so
a new hue gets one without taste deciding it twice?

## Options

In the order tried. The palette as found, `oklch`:

| Hue      | Light             | Dark              |
| -------- | ----------------- | ----------------- |
| success  | `0.646 0.175 147` | `0.732 0.212 148` |
| info     | `0.576 0.219 259` | `0.649 0.193 252` |
| warning  | `0.82 0.16 76`    | the same          |
| critical | `0.625 0.252 23`  | `0.694 0.196 22`  |

1. **One APCA target per theme**, Lc 60 on each theme's ground, solving each hue's lightness. Light
   warning fell to `#be8200`, a brown; the dark's blue and red rose to `#83b2ff` and `#ff928d`,
   pastels, since chroma at that lightness is not there to keep. Lc 45 kept more and lost the same
   way. Contrast measures a hue against a ground; it does not say which lightness is that hue's own.
2. **OKHSL saturation 100, the dark's lightness the light's plus 9**, the pattern the palette's
   pairs measured to: green and red derived within a step of the palette's own. Two exceptions
   stood, warning one value and the dark's blue turned 7 degrees toward cyan.
3. **The same with no exception**, chroma held from falling under seven tenths of the light's. The
   dark's blue went flatter and warning toward cream.
4. **Option 2 with warning its one exception** and information's blue derived like the rest; then,
   seen side by side, **the two themes' values swapped**: the dark read right with the deeper hues
   and the light with the brighter.

## Decision

Option 4, swapped.

- **Saturation 100 in OKHSL in both themes**, so a hue is at its purest whatever its lightness.
- **The dark's lightness is the light's less 9.** In `oklch`, light then dark: success
  `0.724 0.214 147.2` and `0.646 0.191 147.2`, information `0.654 0.185 258.6` and
  `0.576 0.219 258.6`, critical `0.703 0.189 23.1` and `0.625 0.252 23.1`.
- **Warning is one value in both, `0.82 0.161 76`**: a yellow already at its brightest pure point
  turns cream when moved.
- Contrast stays a measure, never a generator: text tiers are set by it, hues are not.
